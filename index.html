How balances work

Opening balance plus income minus expenses determines each account balance. Transfers reduce the source and increase the destination without changing total net worth. Credit-card purchases push the card balance further below zero; card payments move it toward zero. Reconciliation compares cleared activity with an external statement and does not change balances.
